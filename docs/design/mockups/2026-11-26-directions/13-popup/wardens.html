Meet the Wardens

Round two: the Wardens are now scary. They are cut from night paper, with glowing slit eyes, torn edges, and long shadows. They look like the villain of a picture book: no blood, no gore, nothing a child cannot sleep after. Every idea shows the same five Warden Modes: Patrol, Hunt, Intercept, Lured, and defeated.

The current Warden: a purple block with brows. Round one (Hoot, Brick, Grumble) was too friendly. These three replace it.

One badge per Warden Mode

The eye glow changes colour with the mode, and the shoulder badge confirms it. The badge stays the same in every Atlas Region and on every Warden idea.

Warden Guilds for Fangmoor

Each Atlas Region has its own Warden Guild. The wolf stays the same and only the Guild piece and its Region Hue change. Guild art changes no move, timing, or ability.
