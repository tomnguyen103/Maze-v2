Meet your Explorer

Five ideas for the main character, each one a paper cut-out on a stand to fit the Popup book. Each idea shows four moods: ready, Pulse, a wrong answer, and Gate reached.

The current Explorer: a smiley disc. It has no body, no story, and only one mood, so a child cannot tell what it feels or what it can do.

Each idea on a strip of the approved board, at 48 px a tile. The figure stands up out of the page, so it reads over the meadow floor, the cream walls, and the sky Fog flaps.
