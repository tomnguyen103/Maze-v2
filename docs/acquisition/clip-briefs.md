# Clip briefs

Six vertical clips of 15 to 30 seconds. Clips come in pairs. Each pair shows one
demonstration with two different openings, so a channel can compare them. Record
the screen at a mobile width. Show no child's face, voice, name or account. The
owner approves and publishes each clip.

## Links

A Shorts description and a Reels caption do not make a link clickable. Put
`/?c=youtube` in the YouTube channel profile link, or link each Short to the
gameplay explainer video, which carries the link in its description. Put
`/?c=instagram` in the Instagram profile link.

## TikTok

Reuse one adult-facing clip with `/?c=tiktok` only after the owner confirms
account and link eligibility.
