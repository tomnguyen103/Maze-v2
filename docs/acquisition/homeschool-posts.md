## Post 2: Vocabulary in context

A good learning game lets a wrong answer teach something. In Echo Maze, a
wrong answer costs the Explorer some Vitality and brings a fresh Question. A
correct answer clears the Warden from the path.

Here is a real Question from the game:

> A fragile shell must be carried carefully. What does fragile mean?
>
> - easily broken
> - very noisy
> - hard to find

The Hint says to think about why the shell needs gentle hands. After the
answer, the game explains that fragile means easily broken.
