# Reviewer criteria

Use these criteria to choose up to ten reviewer candidates for plan Phase 5.
Keep the candidate list and each contact in the owner's own records, not in
this repository. The owner approves and sends each request.

## A candidate passes when

1. The channel speaks to parents, homeschool families or teachers who choose
   learning games for children.
2. Recent videos review or demonstrate learning tools, not only toys.
3. The audience is adult. The channel is not made for children.
4. The channel discloses sponsored or gifted content.
5. Comments show real questions from parents, not only spam.
6. The channel has a review in the last 90 days.

## A candidate fails when

- The channel targets children directly.
- The channel asks for payment before a review. Paid reviews wait for an owner
  spend cap.
- The channel requires a membership grant before a review. The kit offers none.
- The channel promises results that Echo Maze does not claim, such as grade
  gains.

## The request

Ask for permission to send the gameplay explainer, not for a promised review.
The reviewer plays through the normal Guest Run and the free Explorer account
Runs. Name the one price, `$5.99 USD` once, bought by an adult. Use the reviewer
link `/?c=partner`.
